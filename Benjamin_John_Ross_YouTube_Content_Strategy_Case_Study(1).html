Proposed Strategy

Turning Public Information Into Content People Want to Watch.

A proposed YouTube content strategy for a Singapore public-sector organisation, built around audience-first storytelling, platform-native formats and a connected social content ecosystem.

YouTube StrategyEditorial PlanningSocial ContentAudience BehaviourContent Performance
01 / The Challenge

Information is not automatically engaging.

Public-sector communication often needs to explain initiatives, services and important information clearly. The challenge is translating that information into content that feels relevant, human and easy to consume in a social-first environment.

Strategic opportunity: keep the message accurate and credible while improving the way the story is packaged for the audience.
02 / Audience

Start with how people consume.

Primary audience

Young adults and digitally active Singapore residents who discover information through video and social platforms.

Visual-first
Prefer clear visual explanations over dense information.
Time-poor
Need a strong reason to continue watching.
Social-native
Move between YouTube, Shorts, Instagram and TikTok.
AUDIENCE NEED

“Make it easy to understand.”

Turn complex information into useful, accessible stories.

CONTENT NEED

“Give me a reason to care.”

Connect organisational messages to real people, situations and outcomes.

03 / Content Pillars

Four reasons to press play.

A balanced editorial system keeps the channel useful without making every upload feel like an announcement.

01

EXPLAIN

Explainers, FAQs and how-to formats that make important information easier to understand.

02

INSPIRE

Human stories, community stories and personal journeys that show real outcomes.

03

DISCOVER

Behind-the-scenes stories, lesser-known facts and day-in-the-life content that build curiosity.

04

ENGAGE

Q&A, comment-led topics and community conversations that encourage participation.

04 / Content Ecosystem

One story. Multiple touchpoints.

Build an ecosystem where one strong narrative can be adapted into platform-native formats instead of producing every asset from scratch.

HERO VIDEOYouTube · 8–10 min
SHORTSYouTube · 30–60 sec
REELInstagram · 30–60 sec
TIKTOKNative short-form edit
CAROUSELInstagram · key takeaways
The goal is not to duplicate the same video everywhere. Preserve one core message while adapting the hook, pacing, format and CTA to each platform.

06 / Creative Direction
“The first seconds earn attention. The story earns retention. The message earns trust.”

Hook

Open with a question, tension point or useful promise rather than a formal introduction.

Pacing

Use visual changes, concise scripting and purposeful edits to maintain momentum.

Clarity

Keep language accessible and use visual hierarchy to make key information immediately clear.
